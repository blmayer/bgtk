    fix: terminal — drain PTY, disconnect before munmap on shell exit

    Avoid flooding BGCE with one DRAW per 4K of PTY output, and detach
    from the compositor before munmap so exit/crash of the shell cannot
    race paint (freeze). Reap the child and stop writing after PTY EIO.
