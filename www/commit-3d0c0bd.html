    fix: avoid TinyCC .symtab conflict when linking apps.

    Keep -fPIC only on shared-library objects, stop forcing -Icompat onto
    every lib object, and link real apps against libbgtk.so instead of
    re-merging all PIC .o files into each binary (triggers tcc
    "section type conflict: .symtab 01 <> 02" on launcher and friends).
